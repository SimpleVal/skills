---
name: grill-me
description: Grill the user relentlessly about a plan, decision, or idea. Use when the user wants to stress-test their thinking, or uses any 'grill' trigger phrases.
---

Interview me relentlessly about every aspect of this until we reach a shared understanding.

Map the decisions and their dependencies. Ask each batch of currently independent questions together. Do not include a question whose answer depends on another unanswered question in the same batch.

For each question, provide your recommended answer.

Wait for answers to the entire current batch before continuing. Use those answers to resolve dependencies and form the next batch of newly unblocked questions. There is no fixed batch-size limit; group questions according to their dependencies.

If a fact can be found by exploring the environment, filesystem, or available tools, look it up rather than asking me. The decisions are mine: put each decision to me and wait for my answer.

Do not act on the result until I confirm that we have reached a shared understanding.
